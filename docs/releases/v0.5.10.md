# claude-peer v0.5.10

A claude-peer patch release. It requires Sessionbus v0.5.9 (daemon and hub) and pins the Sessionbus Go SDK `v0.5.9`. Core, the Go SDK and `@sessionbus/kit` remain at v0.5.9. Upgrading from claude-peer v0.5.9 needs no daemon, hub or lane-row change.

Fix:
- **A literal `--` that follows a value-taking option is treated as that option's value.** Native Claude takes the argument after a value-taking option as its value, even when it is `--`, and keeps parsing options after it. In v0.5.9 the managed-tool guard and the typed-field check treated that `--` as the option terminator. So `["--system-prompt", "--", "--disallowedTools", "<managed Sessionbus tool>"]` passed the guard, and with a typed `model` or `permission_mode`, `["--append-system-prompt", "--", "--model", "haiku"]` or `["--append-system-prompt", "--", "--dangerously-skip-permissions"]` passed the typed-field check. These lists are now rejected before launch. A bare `--` still ends the options, and operands after it are not checked.
